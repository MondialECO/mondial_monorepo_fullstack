## 1. Executive Overview: What is Mondial ECO?

**Mondial ECO** is an integrated innovation-to-venture ecosystem engineered to guide visionary concepts from raw initial ideas to institutionally funded, scalable enterprises across Europe and globally.

Traditional startup ecosystems are fragmented: creators lack tools to validate ideas, entrepreneurs struggle to find viable intellectual property, service providers chase unreliable leads, and early-stage investors spend months navigating unstandardized pitch decks. Mondial ECO unites these four key participants into a single digital platform governed by automated phase-based workflows, transparent valuation engines, and application-governed escrow agreements.

## 2. The Four User Personas

Mondial ECO orchestrates the journey of four distinct personas who interact across verified marketplaces and structured deal rooms:

```

                  │               MONDIAL ECO                    │

    │  (Idea/IP)  │        │  (Company)  │ │  (Capital)  │     │ (Services)  │
    └─────────────┘        └─────────────┘ └─────────────┘     └─────────────┘
           │                      ▲                                   ▲

           └────────────────── Hires for Branding ────────────────────┘
```

### 1. The Creator (Idea & IP Originator)
- **Who they are**: Inventors, domain experts, researchers, and creative thinkers with compelling concepts but who may lack the time or operational desire to run a full enterprise.
- **Their Journey**:
  - Validates raw concepts using the **AI Idea Clarifier**.
  - Generates comprehensive brand assets, pitch materials, and 5-year financial forecasts.
  - Reaches **The Crossroads (Phase 5)**: decides between two core directions:
    - **Path A (Marketplace Listing)**: Offers the idea on the public marketplace under two strictly supported deal modes:
      1. **Full Buyout** (`full_buyout`): 100% intellectual property transfer for cash.
      2. **Co-Founder / Equity Partnership** (`equity_partnership`): IP transfer with retained equity (10–49%).
    - **Path B (Build Yourself)**: Private venture spinout involving company formation and seed funding preparation (Level Up). *Architectural rule: Build Yourself is a venture formation path, NOT a marketplace offer.*

### 2. The Entrepreneur (The Builder & Founder)
- **Who they are**: Experienced operators, operators, and builders who excel at execution, hiring, sales, and governance.
- **Their Journey**:
  - Discovers verified, high-scoring Creator projects on the **Project Marketplace** (projected from published `CreatorIdeas`).
  - Acquires vetted intellectual property through structured legal buyout or co-founder contracts.
  - Progresses through the **Phases 1–10 Venture Engine**:
    - **Phase 1**: Identity Verification (Universal Onboarding Gate via Sumsub)
    - **Phase 2**: Company Entity Setup (Incorporation, Team, Industry, Product Stage)
    - **Phase 3**: Traction & Operational KPIs (Monthly Revenue, KPI Tracker)
    - **Phase 4**: Cap Table & ESOP Engineering (Shareholder Allocation, Option Pools, Vesting)
    - **Phase 5**: Valuation & Funding Ask (Multiples, DCF Estimates, Target Raise)
    - **Phase 6**: Data Room & Diligence Readiness (Vault Documents, Automated NDA Gates)
    - **Phase 7**: Automated Investor-Readiness Review (Rules Engine Scoring against Phases 2–6)
    - **Phase 8**: Smart Matchmaking Engine (Ranked Pairing with Qualified Investors)
    - **Phase 9**: Deal Room & Investment Execution (Bilateral Term Sheet Signing, Deal Close)
    - **Phase 10**: Journey Complete (Terminal State, Post-Funding Cap Table & Governance)

### 3. The Investor (The Capital Partner)
- **Who they are**: Angel investors, family offices, and venture capital syndicates looking for high-quality, pre-vetted deal flow.
- **Their Journey**:
  - Completes investor attestation and defines their **Investment Thesis** (sectors, stages, ticket sizes).
  - Receives ranked matches generated by the **Smart Matchmaking Engine**.
  - Signs digital Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) to access audited founder Data Rooms.
  - Formulates digital term sheets, executes deals, and monitors self-reported and deal-driven valuation marks in their personal **Portfolio Holding Dashboard**.

### 4. The Service Provider (The Professional Partner)
- **Who they are**: Specialized agencies, designers, software engineers, legal counsels, and CPA accountants.
- **Their Journey**:
  - Undergoes administrative credential verification to advance along the server-controlled **Tier Ladder**:
    - **Tier 1 — Standard**: Default onboarding tier; standard matching queue.
    - **Tier 2 — Verified**: Credential and profile verification approved; elevated search rank.
    - **Tier 3 — Pro**: Server-side performance, completed volume, and trust score evaluation.
    - **Tier 4 — Elite**: Top-tier quality, dispute-free record, and enterprise client priority.
  - *Platform Commission Policy*: Fixed **flat 12% platform commission** across all engagements and all tiers. Service Provider tiers govern search ranking and matching priority only; they never change pricing or platform commission.
  - Publishes tiered service packages (Basic, Standard, Premium) in the **Service Catalog**.
  - Bids on custom client briefs, delivers work via milestone-based **Workrooms**, and receives payouts tracked through **Platform Escrow** (payment/escrow workflow is modeled in application state; live gateway settlement requires a production payment adapter).

## 3. How the Platform Functions Under the Hood

Mondial ECO is constructed on an enterprise-grade full-stack architecture designed for high availability and strict data confidentiality:

```
[ User Browser ] ────► [ Traefik Proxy (TLS / HTTPS) ] ────► [ Next.js Frontend ]
                                  │
                                  ▼
                     [ ASP.NET Core API Replicas ]
                                  │
                  ┌───────────────┼───────────────┐
                  ▼               ▼               ▼
          [ MongoDB Atlas ]   [ Redis 7 ]   [ Upload Volume ]
```

1. **Next.js 16 Frontend**:
   - Delivers 198 intuitive screens across desktop and mobile.
   - Powered by React 19 and Tailwind CSS 4 to provide instantaneous navigation and dark/light mode themes.
2. **ASP.NET Core 8 Web API**:
   - High-performance, stateless server layer handling 579 distinct business operations.
   - Horizontally scalable: can expand dynamically across multiple containers during peak demand without service interruptions.
3. **MongoDB Atlas Managed Database**:
   - Houses 78 core document collections (81 unique collections across the entire backend including AI session repositories) storing user accounts, financial records, Cap Tables, and AI sessions.
   - Operates with ACID multi-document transactions to guarantee complete data consistency.
4. **Redis 7 Distributed Cache & Backplane**:
   - Coordinates instant real-time chat and alert notifications across all running servers via SignalR Redis backplane.
   - Caches active user presence, distributed cache keys, and secures DataProtection keyrings.

## 4. Key Business Lifecycles

### A. How a Project Becomes a Company
When a Creator and an Entrepreneur finalize an acquisition or partnership on the platform, the intellectual property undergoes an automated transition:
1. The Creator completes all incubation deliverables (Business Plan, Financial Model, IP Documents).
2. The Entrepreneur initiates an acquisition and signs the buyout agreement.
3. Upon payment confirmation and digital asset handover, the platform automatically triggers the **Build-Company Engine** (`POST /api/deals/{id}/build-company`).
4. The system instantiates a new venture profile in `Companies`, maps the Creator's deliverables into the company data room, and advances the entrepreneur directly into Phase 2 of incorporation.

### B. How Venture Funding Works
1. **The Funding Ask**: In Phase 5, the founder uses the algorithmic `ValuationEngine` to determine their company's planning valuation based on current revenue, industry multiples, and market benchmarks (planning estimate only; not a certified financial valuation).
2. **Algorithmic Matchmaking**: The platform's matchmaking engine pairs the venture with qualified investors whose ticket sizes and sector preferences align with the round.
3. **Data Room Diligence**: Investors sign a platform NDA to unlock the founder's audited pitch deck, cap table, and financial reports.
4. **Closing & Capital Ingress**: Both parties digitally execute the Term Sheet and Investment Agreement. Upon wire confirmation, the platform automatically recalculates the company's total capital raised, issues preferred shares on the Cap Table, and creates a live investment asset in the investor's portfolio.

### C. How Escrow Services Protect Workrooms
When a founder hires a Service Provider (e.g. for branding or legal incorporation):
1. The client selects a package or accepts a custom proposal.
2. Escrow funds are recorded into platform state before work begins. *Note: Workroom engagement escrow balances, milestone releases, refunds, and payouts are fully modeled in application domain state (`WorkroomEngagement.EscrowBalance`, `FinancialTransactions`); live financial settlement currently uses the deterministic `StubPaymentGatewayService` adapter and requires connecting a production gateway (e.g., Stripe Connect) for real banking transfers.*
3. The project is broken down into verifiable milestones.
4. The provider uploads deliverables directly to the secure Workroom.
5. The client reviews and accepts the work, automatically releasing milestone funds to the provider's balance in application state. If client review elapses without response, automated platform timers protect the provider by auto-approving submitted deliverables.

## 5. Security & Trust Architecture

1. **Universal Phase 1 Verification**:
   - Every user across all roles must verify their identity via passport/ID inspection and facial liveness verification before interacting with marketplace deals.
2. **Data Room Secrecy**:
   - Sensitive pitch decks, Cap Tables, and contracts are shielded behind cryptographic checks. Static direct downloads are denied by default; files can only be accessed through authenticated endpoints validating active NDA signatures.
3. **Audit Trail & Governance**:
   - Every deal revision, signature, term sheet change, and administrative approval is permanently recorded in tamper-evident `AdminAuditLogs`.
