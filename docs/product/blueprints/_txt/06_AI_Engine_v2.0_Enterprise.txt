06_AI_Engine_v2.0 (Enterprise Specification)
Executive Summary
The AI Engine is the intelligence layer of Mondial.eco. It powers idea discovery, founder intelligence, business planning, matching, reputation scoring and funding readiness through a multi-model AI architecture.
Business Objectives
- Accelerate startup creation
- Improve funding readiness
- Increase matching quality
- Personalize user experiences
- Create defensible platform intelligence
AI Architecture
Core Stack:
- OpenRouter
- OpenAI Models
- Anthropic Models

Future:
- Proprietary Models
- Specialized Startup Intelligence Models
- Private Fine-Tuned Models
Model Routing Strategy
OpenAI:
- Structured generation
- Business planning
- Financial analysis

Anthropic:
- Deep reasoning
- Long-context analysis
- Founder interviews

Router decides model based on task type.
AI Module 1: Idea Generator
Purpose:
Generate startup ideas for users without ideas.

Inputs:
- Skills
- Interests
- Industries
- Goals

Outputs:
- Idea Cards
- Opportunity Scores
- Market Suggestions
AI Module 2: Idea Clarifier
Purpose:
Transform raw ideas into structured opportunities.

Outputs:
- Problem Definition
- Target Audience
- Solution Summary
- Risk Assessment
- Clarity Score
AI Module 3: Founder DNA Engine
Purpose:
Analyze motivations and founder characteristics.

Outputs:
- Builder Type
- Founder Story
- Conviction Score
- Leadership Indicators
AI Module 4: Business Plan Generator
Outputs:
- Executive Summary
- Market Analysis
- Revenue Model
- GTM Strategy
- Operations Plan

Business Rule:
Always editable by user.
AI Module 5: Financial Forecast Engine
Outputs:
- Revenue Forecast
- Cost Forecast
- Cash Flow Projection
- Break-even Analysis

AI results are advisory only.
AI Module 6: Matching Engine
Used For:
- Investor Matching
- Co-Founder Matching
- Service Provider Matching

Inputs:
- Matching Vector
- Skills
- Goals
- Geography
- Reputation
AI Module 7: Reputation Intelligence
Purpose:
Detect trust signals.

Factors:
- Reviews
- Activity
- Verification
- Responsiveness
- Completed Deals
AI Module 8: Funding Readiness Engine
Outputs:
- Funding Readiness Score
- Weakness Analysis
- Recommended Improvements
- Investor Suitability
Prompt Framework
Prompt Layers:
1. System Prompt
2. Product Context
3. User Context
4. Task Prompt
5. Output Formatter

All prompts version controlled.
AI Safety Rules
- No financial guarantees
- No legal guarantees
- No valuation guarantees
- Human review encouraged
- Explain uncertainty when needed
Cost Optimization
- Cache reusable outputs
- Route simple tasks to lower-cost models
- Limit unnecessary regenerations
- Use AI credits for premium features
