10_Permission_Matrix_v2.0 (Enterprise Specification)
Executive Summary
Defines the Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) framework for Mondial.eco, including permissions, restrictions, moderation rights, audit rights and data access policies.
Permission Philosophy
Principles:
- Least Privilege Access
- Role-Based Permissions
- Auditability
- Separation of Duties
- Secure by Default
