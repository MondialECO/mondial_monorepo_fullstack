
06_AI_Engine_v2.0 (Enterprise Specification)
Executive Summary
The AI Engine is the intelligence layer of Mondial.eco. It powers idea discovery, founder intelligence, business planning, matching, reputation scoring and funding readiness through a multi-model AI architecture.
Business Objectives
- Accelerate startup creation- Improve funding readiness- Increase matching quality- Personalize user experiences- Create defensible platform intelligence
AI Architecture
Core Stack:- OpenRouter- OpenAI Models- Anthropic ModelsFuture:- Proprietary Models- Specialized Startup Intelligence Models- Private Fine-Tuned Models
Model Routing Strategy
OpenAI:- Structured generation- Business planning- Financial analysisAnthropic:- Deep reasoning- Long-context analysis- Founder interviewsRouter decides model based on task type.
AI Module 1: Idea Generator
Purpose:Generate startup ideas for users without ideas.Inputs:- Skills- Interests- Industries- GoalsOutputs:- Idea Cards- Opportunity Scores- Market Suggestions
AI Module 2: Idea Clarifier
Purpose:Transform raw ideas into structured opportunities.Outputs:- Problem Definition- Target Audience- Solution Summary- Risk Assessment- Clarity Score
AI Module 3: Founder DNA Engine
Purpose:Analyze motivations and founder characteristics.Outputs:- Builder Type- Founder Story- Conviction Score- Leadership Indicators
AI Module 4: Business Plan Generator
Outputs:- Executive Summary- Market Analysis- Revenue Model- GTM Strategy- Operations PlanBusiness Rule:Always editable by user.
AI Module 5: Financial Forecast Engine
Outputs:- Revenue Forecast- Cost Forecast- Cash Flow Projection- Break-even AnalysisAI results are advisory only.
AI Module 6: Matching Engine
Used For:- Investor Matching- Co-Founder Matching- Service Provider MatchingInputs:- Matching Vector- Skills- Goals- Geography- Reputation
AI Module 7: Reputation Intelligence
Purpose:Detect trust signals.Factors:- Reviews- Activity- Verification- Responsiveness- Completed Deals
AI Module 8: Funding Readiness Engine
Outputs:- Funding Readiness Score- Weakness Analysis- Recommended Improvements- Investor Suitability
Prompt Framework
Prompt Layers:1. System Prompt2. Product Context3. User Context4. Task Prompt5. Output FormatterAll prompts version controlled.
AI Safety Rules
- No financial guarantees- No legal guarantees- No valuation guarantees- Human review encouraged- Explain uncertainty when needed
Cost Optimization
- Cache reusable outputs- Route simple tasks to lower-cost models- Limit unnecessary regenerations- Use AI credits for premium features
Analytics &amp; Monitoring
Track:- Prompt Usage- Token Usage- Model Performance- User Satisfaction- AI Adoption
