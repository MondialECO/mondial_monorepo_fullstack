03_Entrepreneur_Journey_v2.0 (Enterprise Specification)
Executive Summary
The Entrepreneur Journey is the funding and growth engine of Mondial.eco. It supports both existing businesses and startup founders by providing verification, valuation, fundraising, investor matching and due diligence workflows.
Business Objectives
- Help founders become investment-ready.
- Standardize fundraising preparation.
- Reduce investor due diligence friction.
- Increase successful funding outcomes.
- Connect entrepreneurs with investors and execution partners.
Target Users
- Existing Startup Founders
- SME Owners
- Pre-Incorporation Founders
- Creator-to-Entrepreneur Upgrades
- Solo Founders
- Startup Teams
Entrepreneur Entry Paths
Path A: Existing Registered Company
Path B: Startup in Formation
Path C: Creator Upgrade

All paths converge into the Funding Readiness Workflow.
Stage 1: Verification & Onboarding
Requirements:
- Identity Verification
- KYC
- Company Information
- Business Classification
- Industry Selection

Outputs:
- Verified Entrepreneur Profile
- Trust Score
Stage 2: Company & Startup Profile
Fields:
- Company Name
- Registration Status
- Website
- Industry
- Team Size
- Revenue Stage
- Growth Stage

Outputs:
- Company Profile
- Startup Snapshot
Stage 3: Funding Readiness Assessment
AI Evaluation:
- Team Readiness
- Product Readiness
- Market Readiness
- Financial Readiness

Outputs:
- Funding Readiness Score
- Gap Analysis
- Recommended Actions
Stage 4: Valuation Engine
Inputs:
- Revenue
- Growth Rate
- Users
- Market Size
- Industry Benchmarks

Outputs:
- Estimated Valuation Range
- Investor Presentation Metrics

Business Rule:
Valuation is advisory, not legally binding.
Stage 5: Cap Table Management
Features:
- Founder Ownership
- Co-Founder Shares
- Employee Allocation
- ESOP Pool
- Investor Equity

Outputs:
- Current Cap Table
- Dilution Simulation
Stage 6: Funding Request Creation
Funding Information:
- Amount Requested
- Equity Offered
- SAFE Terms
- Use of Funds
- Milestones

Outputs:
- Funding Listing
- Investor Visibility
Stage 7: Data Room
Storage:
- Pitch Deck
- Business Plan
- Financial Forecast
- Legal Documents
- Cap Table
- Team Information

Access Control:
Investor approval and NDA workflow.
Stage 8: Investor Matching
Matching Factors:
- Industry
- Geography
- Stage
- Ticket Size
- Thesis Alignment
- Risk Profile

Outputs:
- Match Score
- Investor Recommendations
Stage 9: Fundraising Pipeline
Stages:

- Published
