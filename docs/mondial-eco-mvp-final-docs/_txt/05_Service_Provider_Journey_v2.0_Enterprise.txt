05_Service_Provider_Journey_v2.0 (Enterprise Specification)
Executive Summary
The Service Provider Journey is the execution engine of Mondial.eco. It connects founders and businesses with verified professionals who can help execute projects, deliver services and support startup growth.
Business Objectives
- Enable startup execution
- Create trusted service transactions
- Improve founder success rates
- Standardize project delivery workflows
- Generate marketplace revenue
Supported Categories
1. Development
2. Design
3. Marketing
4. Legal
5. Finance
6. Accounting
7. Operations
8. Strategy
9. Due Diligence
10. Fundraising Support
11. AI Automation
12. HR / Recruitment

Requirements:
- Identity Verification
- Professional Information
- Skills Verification
- Portfolio Submission

- Task Tracking

→ Milestone Delivery
→ Approval

Funds remain protected until approval conditions are met.
