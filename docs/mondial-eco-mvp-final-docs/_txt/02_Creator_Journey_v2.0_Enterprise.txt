02_Creator_Journey_v2.0 (Enterprise Specification)
Executive Summary
The Creator Journey is the core value engine of Mondial.eco. It helps users transform ideas into validated, structured and investor-ready opportunities through AI-assisted workflows.
Business Objectives
- Help users discover or refine ideas.
- Increase startup creation success rates.
- Generate investor-ready documentation.
- Create a repeatable founder journey.
- Feed the Entrepreneur and Funding engines.
Target Users
- First-time founders
- Innovators
- Students
- Professionals with ideas
- Aspiring startup builders
Journey Overview
Authentication → KYC → Intelligence Profile → Idea Discovery → Idea Clarifier → Idea Journal → Business Plan → Financial Forecast → IP Vault → Crossroads
Stage 1: Onboarding
Requirements:
- Account Creation
- Email Verification
- KYC
- User Intelligence Profile

Output:
Verified Creator Profile
Stage 2: Idea Discovery
Entry Paths:
A. I Have an Idea
B. I Need an Idea

Features:
- AI Idea Cards
- Trend Analysis
- Industry Discovery
- Problem Discovery

Output:
Selected Idea Concept
Stage 3: AI Idea Clarifier
AI Interview:
- Problem Statement
- Target Audience
- Pain Points
- Existing Alternatives
- Proposed Solution

Outputs:
- Clarity Score
- Idea Summary
- Opportunity Assessment
- Risk Assessment
Stage 4: Idea Journal
Stores:
- Conversations
- Idea Revisions
- AI Outputs
- Notes
- Attachments

Business Rule:
Nothing is deleted permanently without archive.
Stage 5: Business Plan Generator
Sections:
- Executive Summary
- Market Analysis
- Competitor Analysis
- Revenue Model
- Go-To-Market Strategy
- Operations Plan

Output:
Investor-Ready Business Plan
Stage 6: Financial Forecast
Generate:
- Revenue Projections
- Cost Estimates
- Cash Flow Forecast
- Break-even Analysis

Output:
Funding Preparation Package
Stage 7: IP Vault
Purpose:
Protect founder work.

Stores:
- Idea Snapshots
- Timestamps
- Business Plans
- Supporting Documents

Output:
Ownership Evidence Trail
Stage 8: Crossroads
Option A:
Sell Idea

Option B:
Build Startup

Option C:
Upgrade to Entrepreneur Profile
