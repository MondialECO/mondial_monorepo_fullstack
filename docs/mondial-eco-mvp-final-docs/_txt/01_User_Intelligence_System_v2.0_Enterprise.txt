01_User_Intelligence_System_v2.0 (Enterprise Specification)
Executive Summary
The User Intelligence System is the core intelligence layer of Mondial.eco. It transforms every user profile into a structured intelligence profile that powers matching, AI personalization, funding readiness, reputation scoring and collaboration recommendations.
Business Objectives
1. Understand users beyond basic profiles.
2. Improve founder-investor matching.
3. Improve service provider recommendations.
4. Generate personalized AI outputs.
5. Create a long-term intelligence graph for the ecosystem.
Phase 1 - Identity & Verification Layer
Purpose: Establish trust.

Fields:
- Full Name
- Display Name
- Email
- Phone
- Country
- City
- Timezone
- Languages
- Verification Status

Business Rules:
- Email verification required.
- KYC required for funding features.
- Multiple accounts prohibited.

Outputs:
- Identity Score
- Verification Status
Phase 2 - Experience & Skill Intelligence
Purpose: Understand professional capability.

Experience:
- Current Role
- Years of Experience
- Industry Experience
- Startup Experience

Skill Graph:
- Primary Skills (max 5)
- Secondary Skills (max 10)
- Skill Confidence (1-5)

AI Outputs:
- Skill Strength Map
- Skill Gap Analysis
- Recommended Collaborators
Phase 3 - Founder DNA & Goal Engine
Purpose: Understand intent and motivation.

Questions:
- Why are you building?
- What problem are you solving?
- What motivates you?
- What is your 5-year vision?

Outputs:
- Founder DNA
- Mission Statement
- Vision Statement
- Conviction Score
- Builder Archetype

Goal Engine:
- Build Startup
- Raise Funding
- Find Investors
- Hire Talent
- Offer Services
- Invest in Startups
Phase 4 - Collaboration & Reputation Engine
Collaboration Profile:
- Looking For
- Work Style
- Communication Preference
- Availability

Reputation Inputs:
- Verification
- Reviews
- Completed Deals
- Responsiveness
- Platform Activity

Output:
Reputation Score (0-100)
