00_Master_Product_Blueprint_v2.0 (Enterprise Edition - Expanded)
1. Executive Summary
Mondial.eco is a Founder-to-Funding Platform powered by Project Intelligence.
The platform helps creators, founders, startups, SMEs and investors move from idea,
validation, planning, funding and execution through a single ecosystem.
2. Vision
Enable anyone with an idea, startup or business to become funding-ready through AI-powered intelligence,
structured execution and trusted investment workflows.
3. Mission
Reduce friction between ideas, founders, investors and execution partners.
4. Product Positioning
Official Positioning:

- Freelancer Marketplace
- Startup Directory
