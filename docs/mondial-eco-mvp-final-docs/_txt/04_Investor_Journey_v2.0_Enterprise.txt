04_Investor_Journey_v2.0 (Enterprise Specification)
Executive Summary
The Investor Journey is the capital allocation engine of Mondial.eco. It enables Angel Investors, Venture Capital firms and Family Offices to discover, evaluate, manage and invest in high-potential opportunities through structured workflows and intelligence-driven matching.
Business Objectives
- Increase qualified investor-founder matches
- Reduce due diligence time
- Improve deal quality
- Create a trusted investment ecosystem
- Standardize investment workflows
Investor Types
- Angel Investors
- Venture Capital Firms
- Family Offices

Future:
- Syndicates
- Corporate VC
- Institutional Investors
