
02_Creator_Journey_v2.0 (Enterprise Specification)
Executive Summary
The Creator Journey is the core value engine of Mondial.eco. It helps users transform ideas into validated, structured and investor-ready opportunities through AI-assisted workflows.
Business Objectives
- Help users discover or refine ideas.- Increase startup creation success rates.- Generate investor-ready documentation.- Create a repeatable founder journey.- Feed the Entrepreneur and Funding engines.
Target Users
- First-time founders- Innovators- Students- Professionals with ideas- Aspiring startup builders
Journey Overview
Authentication → KYC → Intelligence Profile → Idea Discovery → Idea Clarifier → Idea Journal → Business Plan → Financial Forecast → IP Vault → Crossroads
Stage 1: Onboarding
Requirements:- Account Creation- Email Verification- KYC- User Intelligence ProfileOutput:Verified Creator Profile
Stage 2: Idea Discovery
Entry Paths:A. I Have an IdeaB. I Need an IdeaFeatures:- AI Idea Cards- Trend Analysis- Industry Discovery- Problem DiscoveryOutput:Selected Idea Concept
Stage 3: AI Idea Clarifier
AI Interview:- Problem Statement- Target Audience- Pain Points- Existing Alternatives- Proposed SolutionOutputs:- Clarity Score- Idea Summary- Opportunity Assessment- Risk Assessment
Stage 4: Idea Journal
Stores:- Conversations- Idea Revisions- AI Outputs- Notes- AttachmentsBusiness Rule:Nothing is deleted permanently without archive.
Stage 5: Business Plan Generator
Sections:- Executive Summary- Market Analysis- Competitor Analysis- Revenue Model- Go-To-Market Strategy- Operations PlanOutput:Investor-Ready Business Plan
Stage 6: Financial Forecast
Generate:- Revenue Projections- Cost Estimates- Cash Flow Forecast- Break-even AnalysisOutput:Funding Preparation Package
Stage 7: IP Vault
Purpose:Protect founder work.Stores:- Idea Snapshots- Timestamps- Business Plans- Supporting DocumentsOutput:Ownership Evidence Trail
Stage 8: Crossroads
Option A:Sell IdeaOption B:Build StartupOption C:Upgrade to Entrepreneur Profile
UX Requirements
- Multi-step workflow- Auto-save- Progress tracking- Resume sessions- Mobile-first experience
Validation Rules
Required:- Idea Title- Problem Statement- Target AudienceOptional:- Attachments- References
