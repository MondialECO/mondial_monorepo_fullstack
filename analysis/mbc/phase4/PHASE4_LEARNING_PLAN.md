# PHASE 4.4 — LEARNING PLAN SPECIFICATION & TAXONOMY

## Purpose & Scope
The Learning Plan component within Phase 4.4 translates capability requirements assigned to the `LEARN` resolution path into structured, time-budgeted, actionable learning curricula.

---

## Non-Prescriptive AI Avoidance
Rather than generating open-ended or arbitrary AI curricula, Phase 4.4 relies on a curated capability taxonomy. Each curated topic provides:
1. **Practical Business Objective**: Framed around venture delivery rather than abstract theory.
2. **Current vs. Target Level**: Clear proficiency goals (`Foundational`, `Comfortable`, `Advanced`).
3. **Estimated Learning Hours**: Realistic time allocations calibrated against weekly availability.
4. **Curated Action Topics**: 2 to 4 high-impact, actionable skill domains.
5. **Recommended Format**: Self-guided, intensive workshop, or modular micro-learning.

## Time Budget Calibration & Availability Bounds
The engine automatically evaluates the creator's declared weekly availability from HumainX:
- **High Availability (> 30 hrs/week)**: Can absorb up to 2 simultaneous medium-complexity `LEARN` actions in parallel.
- **Moderate Availability (15–30 hrs/week)**: Staggers learning actions sequentially across the Roadmap 30/60/90 day timeline.
- **Constrained Availability (< 15 hrs/week)**: Prioritizes `DELEGATE` for heavy technical tasks (> 20 hours estimated) and restricts `LEARN` to foundational literacy only (< 8 hours).
