## 4. Key Takeaways on Reuse

1. **85% of Infrastructure Already Exists:** MBC has mature data stores, AI execution pipelines (`OpenRouterClient`), credit metering, physical file storage (`CreatorIdeaDocument`), and scoring engines (`ComputeReadiness`).
2. **Zero Need for External Microservices:** Legal & Compliance Intelligence can be cleanly embedded into the existing ASP.NET Core backend and Next.js frontend without introducing Python services, external vector databases, or complex scrapers.
3. **Core Gaps are Domain-Specific:** The only genuinely missing pieces are:
   - The structured France Legal Rule Catalogue with official source citations.
   - The deterministic applicability evaluation engine.
   - The 3-pane Legal Workspace UI layout.
   - The Level-Up data carry-forward from Creator evidence to Entrepreneur Company documents.
