## 1. Documentation Authority Hierarchy & Baseline Rules

Per `docs/DOCUMENTATION-AUTHORITY.md`, the repository enforces strict governance for resolving discrepancies:
1. **AS-IS Code Always Trumps Docs:** If any document contradicts current compiled backend (`backend/`) or frontend (`src/`) code, the document is in drift and the code governs.
2. **Canonical Documents Trump Supporting Docs:** Documents marked `CANONICAL` or `CANONICAL (LOCKED)` govern over secondary architecture docs.
3. **Product Blueprints are Non-Normative for AS-IS:** Files in `docs/product/blueprints/` represent target future-state product vision and must not be cited as current production behavior.
4. **Archive is Strictly Non-Normative:** Files in `docs/archive/` carry zero authority and are preserved strictly for historical lineage.

## 3. Key Architectural Conflicts & Locked Decisions Identified in Documentation

### A. The "Phase 3 Closure" Rule vs Feature Scope
- **Documented Decision:** In `docs/product/creator-flow-canon.md` (§5, §11), Phase 3 was declared **CLOSED and STABLE** on September 18, 2026. The rule states:
  > *"Architecture frozen against new features or redesigns; critical bug fixes only."*
- **Conflict Analysis:** Adding a rich, multi-tiered "Legal & Compliance Intelligence" workspace directly into Phase 3 could be viewed as a breach of Phase 3 closure if framed as a net-new step or structural redesign.
- **Resolution Path:** The canon explicitly permits *"defect repair against spec"* and acknowledges that Step 3.5 (`/phase-3/compliance`) currently operates as a thin heuristic placeholder (12 hardcoded items, basic FinTech switch, zero real France statutory rules). Upgrading Step 3.5 to a genuine Legal & Compliance Intelligence engine must be architected as the **completion and deep-hardening of Step 3.5** within the existing 7-step sequence, strictly preserving the `/dashboard/creator/phase-3/compliance` route and the 7-step milestone structure.

### B. Advisory Guidance vs Mandatory Gate
- **Documented Decision (2026-07-24):** In `docs/product/creator-flow-canon.md` (§5.3, §5.7, §11), the Legal Checklist gate was demoted to advisory guidance:
  > *"Phase 3 completes on plan + forecast + formation; mandatory legal items no longer block the derivation engine or the masterplan endpoint... pure self-attestation - the gate produced checkbox-cycling friction, not assurance."*
- **Impact on Target Architecture:** The new Legal & Compliance Intelligence engine must NOT reintroduce a rigid, artificial blocker preventing founders from progressing to Phase 4. Instead, it must compute an accurate **"Legal Planning Readiness"** score (which directly feeds the 15-point `LegalReadiness` dimension in Step 3.7 Investor Readiness Audit) while keeping the step advanceable as informed guidance.

### C. AI Provider Consolidation vs Blueprint References
- **Documented Decision:** In `docs/product/creator-flow-canon.md` (§1) and `docs/DOCUMENTATION-AUTHORITY.md` (§27), MBC consolidated on a single AI provider: **OpenRouter** (`google/gemini-3.8-flash`). Older blueprints (`06_AI_Engine_v2.0_Enterprise.txt`) mentioning Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet or local Ollama instances are formally superseded.
- **Impact on Target Architecture:** Any generative explanation, legal synthesis, or clarification processing must execute via `OpenRouterClient` through the standard `IAiTaskHandler` and Hangfire queue.

### D. Single Source of Truth for Legal Rules (No AI Invention)
- **Documented Canon:** In `docs/product/creator-flow-canon.md` (§1.8 Honest-Labelling) and `backend/Services/Ai/Prompts/SafetyRules.cs`:
  > *"Do not provide guarantees about financial returns, valuations, funding outcomes, or legal/tax/regulatory matters... Express uncertainty honestly; never invent figures, sources, citations, or facts."*
- **Impact on Target Architecture:** AI must NEVER invent French statutes, articles, or compliance requirements. The rule catalogue must be **authoritatively deterministic** (curated from official French sources: INPI, CNIL, Service-Public, URSSAF, DGCCRF), while AI is strictly restricted to contextual synthesis, plain-language simplification, and business-plan drafting.
